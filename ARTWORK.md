## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: landscape marketplace cover for an independent Omarchy community plugin, 1536 by 1024 composition.
Primary request: a cool, polished illustration for Omarchy Community Knowledge — a shared notebook of computer fixes so every person's agent can learn from what other people solved.
Subject: an open luminous notebook made from neat circuit traces, with a small laptop and a few simple linked knowledge cards integrated into one clear central composition. Suggest useful knowledge being shared, not surveillance or a giant server infrastructure.
Style/medium: sophisticated retro-computing editorial illustration, subtle pixel-inspired geometry and tactile screenprint texture, crisp strong silhouette and generous breathing room; restrained luminous accents on a dark background.
Text (verbatim): "OMARCHY" with "COMMUNITY KNOWLEDGE" below it in clean, bold, beautifully spaced readable typography. Spell OMARCHY exactly O-M-A-R-C-H-Y. No other text.
Constraints: independent community project, no official Omarchy logo, no endorsement badge, no fake UI screenshot, no watermark, no stock-photo people; readable when reduced to a marketplace card.
